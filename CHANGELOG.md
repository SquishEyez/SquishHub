# Changelog

## 1.4.0 (first public release)

The all-in-one hub for the SquishEyez collection (`ssquisheyezz`) and $SQUISH
on WAX, for Android and Linux.

- **F1 Hub:** wrap 69 $SQUISH into a CD-R NFT and back, fuse Season 2 Shardz,
  open packs
- **F2 Drops:** every SquishEyez drop on NeftyBlocks and NFTHive, including
  claiming at a drop's second price
- **F3 Farms:** stake, unstake and claim $SQUISH on the SquishEyez farms
- **F4 Vote:** SquishDAO, one vote per OG / Season 2 SquishEyez, nothing staked
- **F5 Bag:** your $SQUISH, WAX and SquishEyez NFTs
- **F6 Sk8:** the SquishEyez × Sk8Coin collab: your SKATE balance, every part
  (deck, wheels, trucks, bearings) with its live drops, and building a
  skateboard
- The $SQUISH window on every page: supply against the 963,369 cap
- Sign in with Anchor or WAX Cloud Wallet
- New: Linux desktop app (`.deb` and AppImage)
- Android: signed with the permanent SquishHub release key; cloud backup off
